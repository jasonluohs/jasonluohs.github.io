About

Hello, I'm Hongsen.

I'm an undergraduate at the University of Science and Technology of China (School of the Gifted Young), majoring in Artificial Intelligence and Data Science.

I like building things end-to-end: in the past year I've led perception and control for a competition robot, built an agent that writes Slurm job scripts for HPC clusters, and explored how LLM agents can drive real scientific software like VASP.

What ties these together is a question I keep coming back to: how far can a system go from consuming knowledge to generating and testing its own scientific hypotheses? That question is what this site is for — it's a running record of what I build, learn, and wonder.

Research Interests

AI for Science · Scientific Agents · Embodied Intelligence · Agentic AI · Representation Learning
