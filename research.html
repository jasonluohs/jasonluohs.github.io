Research

What I'm curious about.

This page is less a list of papers than a record of the direction I'm trying to form. As an undergraduate, what matters most right now is developing my own question consciousness — so I keep the questions here, and let the answers accumulate over time.

Q1

Can AI Scientists formulate genuinely useful scientific questions?

Existing AI4Science systems often focus on solving predefined problems. I'm interested in the upstream process: how can an agent identify gaps in existing knowledge and propose experimentally testable hypotheses?
Q2

Can agents continuously learn from experiments?

knowledge → hypothesis → experiment → observation → update → ↻
This loop motivates my interest in autonomous experimentation and continual learning. Most current agents treat each task as isolated; the interesting question is what accumulates between tasks.
Q3

How should scientific knowledge be represented for agents?

My work on VASP Router made this concrete for me: an agent is only as good as the structure of the knowledge it can act on.
  • Knowledge graphs over scientific literature
  • Physical constraints as guardrails
  • Causal relationships vs. correlations
  • Shaping the hypothesis space itself
